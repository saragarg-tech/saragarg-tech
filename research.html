Collaborative Radiology AI Research

In partnership with researchers from Mayo Clinic and Emory Radiology, this project evaluates the efficacy of extracting granular pathology labels from radiology reports using Large Language Models to improve automated chest radiograph analysis.

Technical Summary

  • Focus: Granular pathology extraction & disease classification
  • Methodology: LLM-extracted labeling pipelines paired with deep convolutional networks
  • Conference: SIIM24 Annual Meeting & Informatic TECH Expo

Potency Prediction Models

Comparative analysis evaluating the performance gap between classical QSAR models and quantum computing frameworks in predicting therapeutic compound potency.

Technical Summary

  • Status: Accepted to ACS Medicinal Chemistry Letters (2026)
  • Domain: Computational chemistry, drug discovery pipelines, quantum machine learning

Biological Age Disparities as Disease Predictors

Investigating discrepancies between chronological age and biological age derived from imaging metadata and epigenetic clocks to predict chronic disease trajectories.

Technical Summary

  • Status: Forthcoming Publication
  • Impact: Identifying systemic disparities in biological aging markers across diverse patient cohorts
